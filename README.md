# 🛋️ IKEA Customer Analytics Dashboard | SQL| PYTHON | POWER BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **IKEA Customer Analytics Dashboard** is a Power BI project designed to analyze customer behavior, revenue performance, and customer retention using a simulated retail dataset.

The project focuses on understanding how different customer segments contribute to revenue, identifying churn patterns, and using **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis** to uncover opportunities for customer retention and business growth.

The dashboard is divided into two main sections: **Business Overview** and **RFM Analysis**, providing a clear view of overall business performance and customer purchasing behavior.

> **Note:** This project uses simulated data for analytical and educational purposes. The results do not represent IKEA's actual business performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze revenue trends across years, quarters, and months.
- Understand revenue contribution from different customer segments.
- Evaluate customer churn and retention patterns.
- Segment customers using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary metrics.
- Identify high-value customers and customers who may require re-engagement.
- Generate actionable business recommendations from the analysis.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** — Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation.
- **DAX** — Calculated measures and business KPIs.
- **RFM Analysis** — Customer segmentation based on purchasing behavior.
- **SQL Studio** - For data cleaning and combining it
- **Pandas** - To do thorough detail oriented data analysis using pandas.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 📈 1. Business Overview

This section provides a high-level view of customer and revenue performance through key performance indicators and interactive visualizations.

- **Total Customers:** 5,881
- **Total Revenue:** $17.4M
- **Customer Churn Rate:** 50.86%
- **Average Customer Value:** 2,954

The dashboard includes revenue trends over time, quarterly performance, and revenue contribution by customer segment.

### 👥 2. RFM Analysis

RFM analysis categorizes customers according to three behavioral metrics:

- **Recency (R):** How recently a customer made a purchase.
- **Frequency (F):** How frequently a customer purchases.
- **Monetary (M):** How much a customer contributes in monetary value.

The dashboard groups customers into six segments:

| Customer Segment | Customers | Revenue |
|---|---:|---:|
| Champions | 1,291 | $11.84M |
| Loyal | 1,416 | $2.70M |
| At Risk | 821 | $1.59M |
| Lost | 1,524 | $0.65M |
| New | 442 | $0.39M |
| Needs Attention | 387 | $0.20M |

These segments help distinguish high-value customers from customers who may need retention campaigns or reactivation strategies.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **🏆 Champions drive revenue:** The Champions segment contributes approximately $11.84M, making it the largest revenue-generating customer segment.
- **💎 Loyal customers offer growth potential:** Loyal customers contribute approximately $2.70M and represent an opportunity for cross-selling, upselling, and continued engagement.
- **⚠️ Customer churn needs attention:** The dashboard reports an overall churn rate of 50.86%, highlighting the importance of investigating customer inactivity and retention opportunities.
- **🔄 Lost customers present a reactivation opportunity:** The Lost segment contains 1,524 customers but contributes approximately $0.65M in revenue.
- **📅 Q4 leads quarterly revenue:** Q4 generates approximately $6.2M, exceeding the other quarters in the displayed period.
- **📈 Revenue increased significantly after 2009:** Annual revenue rises from approximately $0.7M in 2009 to $8.4M in 2010, followed by a slight decrease to approximately $8.3M in 2011.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- **🎁 Reward high-value customers:** Introduce loyalty benefits, personalized offers, and exclusive promotions for Champions.
- **🤝 Strengthen customer relationships:** Encourage repeat purchases among Loyal customers through relevant recommendations and loyalty programs.
- **📩 Re-engage inactive customers:** Use targeted email campaigns, personalized discounts, and win-back offers for Lost and At Risk customers.
- **🛒 Improve new customer conversion:** Encourage first-time buyers to make repeat purchases through onboarding campaigns and product recommendations.
- **📊 Investigate churn patterns:** Examine customer inactivity, purchasing frequency, and segment-level behavior to identify possible retention issues.
- **📆 Plan for seasonal demand:** Investigate the factors behind stronger Q4 revenue and use those findings to support inventory and marketing decisions.
